One last step: add the extension

The helper is installed. Your browser only adds extensions from outside its store when you load them yourself, which takes a minute.

  1. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions (copy it into the address bar; browsers don't open this page from links).
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
  4. Pin YT Tab Downloader from the puzzle-piece menu, open a YouTube video and click the icon.

Keep the folder where it is: the browser loads the extension from it. After updating YT Media Harvester, click Reload on the extension's card. To remove everything, uninstall YT Media Harvester from Windows Settings and remove the extension from the browser.
