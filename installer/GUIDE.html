One last step: add the extension

The helper is installed. Browsers only let you add this extension yourself, so here's how. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open your browser's extensions page by typing it into the address bar: chrome://extensions, edge://extensions or brave://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode. It's a switch in the top right in Chrome and Brave, and on the left in Edge.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder that the installer just opened. It's in the folder where YT Media Harvester was installed.

Then open a YouTube video, click the red YT Tab Downloader icon and pick MP3 or MP4. Files go to Downloads\YT Media Harvester; you can change that under Settings.

If the popup says "Helper offline", run the installer again and tick your browser. To remove everything, use "Add or remove programs" in Windows Settings.
